1-year warranty

Every StashPi order is covered against defects for a full year from the delivery date.

What's covered

For 1 year from the date your order is delivered, we'll repair or replace, at our discretion, any of the following if it fails under normal use due to a manufacturing or workmanship defect:

  • The Raspberry Pi board (full kits only)
  • The microSD card, including a card ordered on its own under "card only"
  • Any add-ons purchased with your order (power bank, Faraday bag, USB drive)
If your microSD card develops errors or your device won't boot within the warranty period, we'll also re-flash or replace it with your original library selection at no charge — you don't need to reconfigure anything.

What's not covered

  • Physical damage from drops, liquid, or improper power supplies
  • Damage from unauthorized hardware modification
  • Normal wear on connectors and cables
  • A Raspberry Pi you supplied yourself under a "card only" order — that hardware is covered by its original manufacturer's warranty, not ours

This warranty covers hardware only. The libraries and software are provided as-is; where they're built on open-source components (see tech specs), those components carry no warranty of their own, consistent with their open-source licenses.

How to make a claim

  1. Email us your order reference (the "SP-…" code from your confirmation) and a description of the issue.
  2. We'll confirm the issue is covered and, if a physical unit needs to be sent back, provide return instructions.
  3. We'll repair, replace, or re-flash your unit and ship it back at no charge.
