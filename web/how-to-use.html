How to use StashPi

Everything from first boot to browsing your own files, in the order you'll actually do it.

1. First boot

Insert the microSD card (already flashed if you ordered a full kit or a pre-flashed card) and connect power. There's no power switch — plugging in power turns it on, and unplugging it turns it off.

StashPi boots in well under a minute. Once it's up, it starts broadcasting its own WiFi network — you don't need a router, a SIM card, or any existing internet connection.

Nothing to configure on first boot. There's no setup wizard, no account to create, and no software to install on your devices.

2. Connect to the network

On your phone, tablet, or laptop, open WiFi settings and join the network named StashPi. It's an open network — no password required.

Once connected, open any web browser and go to:

http://1.1.1.1

This works from any device with a browser — phones, tablets, laptops, Chromebooks — no app install needed.

3. Browse and search the library

You'll land on the library home page, showing a tile for each reference collection you selected when ordering (Wikipedia, maps, medical references, and so on). Tap a tile to open it.

Inside each library, use the search bar the same way you would online — it's fully indexed on the device, so results come back instantly with no internet required.

A "Main Page" button always returns you to that library's home/contents page from anywhere inside it.

4. Browse a USB drive

StashPi can also browse files from a USB drive plugged directly into the Pi — not your phone or laptop's own storage, but a drive physically inserted into the device.

  1. Plug a USB drive (FAT32, exFAT, NTFS, or ext4 formatted) into one of the Pi's USB ports.
  2. Wait a few seconds for it to be detected and mounted automatically.
  3. On the library home page, open the USB Drive tile.
  4. Browse folders and preview files — images, PDFs, text, audio, and video — from any device connected to the network.
The drive is mounted read-only: StashPi can display your files but can never modify or delete anything on it. Unplugging the drive removes the tile until another one is inserted.

5. Play chess

A lightweight chess game ships on every build, listed as its own tile on the library home page. Play against a built-in AI opponent, or pass the device between two players.

6. Turning it off

Simply unplug the power. There's no shutdown sequence required — the device doesn't hold any unsaved state, since it's a read-only reference library.

Troubleshooting

I don't see the "StashPi" WiFi network

Give it a full minute after powering on. If it still doesn't appear, confirm the device has power (most builds show an activity light) and that you're within a normal WiFi range — a few rooms or roughly 30 feet indoors.

The page won't load at 1.1.1.1

Make sure your device is actually joined to the "StashPi" network, not a previously remembered network with a similar name. Some phones auto-disconnect from open WiFi networks without internet access — check your WiFi settings if the connection seems to drop.

My USB drive doesn't show up

Confirm it's formatted as FAT32, exFAT, NTFS, or ext4. Try a different USB port, and give it 10–15 seconds after inserting. If it still doesn't appear, the drive's filesystem may be unsupported or damaged.
